## Consequences to apply

- D1: remove the open question and the `self_approval_allowed` parameter from SPD. A group with one person who can approve cannot spend until a second person holds the permission.
- D3: the earlier default (own-wallet moves and rule-made entries are not income) is replaced. Because every arrival is income, rules can chain from wallet to wallet. Cycle detection (RUL-06) and a maximum chain depth become mandatory, and each entry in a chain carries the name of the rule that made it.

- D4: SPD-09 and VOT-03 no longer send the expense to approvers after a vote. The vote result starts the payout, after the SPD-10 safety checks (balance, freeze, cap, quorum met on the final result). The floor "no single person moves the money alone" is met by the vote itself, so quorum and majority settings must never allow a single voter to decide a money vote: a money vote needs at least two distinct voters other than the requester (default to confirm).
- D5: the leftover is a visible ledger entry, labelled as a rounding amount credited to Gereo, always under one ariary per destination, shown on the receipt and written into the terms the person accepts (ACC-13). It must be a documented platform income line.

## Offline and queue (D6)

Owner: XCT epic. XCT-05 owns the queue rules, XCT-04 owns the cache, XCT-06 owns money-out being online only. Parameters: WAL owns `PAYOUT_PROVIDER_WAIT_MAX`, OPS-13 owns `PAYOUT_PENDING_ALERT_MINUTES`, the XCT parameter table owns the `QUEUE_*` parameters.

Registries in XCT-05:
- Two registries and one informational "Never queued" table. Every registry row names the owner story, the server endpoint and a side-effect class (ledger, approval, membership, permission, creation, authorisation, none).
- Auto-send registry: items that send themselves when the app is open with signal. Ballot, RSVP, personal date or note, task create, update, status and note, decision attachment upload, proof attachment added to an already-sent request, support-ticket message, Report this payment, manual decision entry, decision amendment, notification preference changes and read marks, KYC photo upload, invite accept.
- Local-drafts registry: items saved on the phone and never sent without a tap. They are not queue items. Expense request draft, announcement draft, group form draft, group edit draft including rules text, vote-creation draft.
- The guard test fails any Auto-send row whose class is not `none`, except one named allowlisted class `membership-self`, which contains invite accept only.
- Default-deny stays the enforceable rule. "Never queued" is a readable index that cites owners (XCT-06 criterion 1 for money-out). It is not a second source of truth.
- Message keys: money-out blocked uses `xct.offline.money_blocked` (XCT-06). Every other non-queueable action uses `xct.offline.needs_connection`. The `wal.offline.*` keys, the RUL keys and ad-hoc "Sending needs a connection" keys are swept into these two.

Ballots (consistent with D4):
- Queue state Confirmed is shown to the voter as `Received`. `Counted` appears only in results, and only for ballots that count toward quorum.
- A ballot is one per (voter, vote). The server decides by receipt order, never client time. At receipt the server re-checks the Vote permission and the membership.
- Guest voters on the light vote page cannot queue. Their ballot is not queueable.
- Vote by SMS is Later.

Fourth guard class, authorisation (consistent with D4):
- Manual decision entries and amendments can never serve as the authorising vote of a payout and never count toward quorum or approver counts.
- They keep the badge `Recorded by {name}, not a vote` through amendments, and the original text stays visible.

Retry and expiry:
- Only attempts made while connected count against `QUEUE_MAX_RETRIES`. Backoff resets when connectivity returns and when the app opens or returns to the foreground.
- Ballots keep retrying until their deadline.
- New parameter `QUEUE_ITEM_MAX_DAYS` (7) expires any other Auto-send item with a visible message. KYC photos use `QUEUE_KYC_MAX_DAYS` (3).
- The PWA cannot send when closed, so every Waiting to send item says "Open Gereo when you have signal".

Storage and KYC photos:
- Queued KYC photos: in-app capture only, encrypted with a non-extractable device key (the limit of this protection is documented honestly), at most 10 KYC cases per device.
- They are deleted when the server confirms, on sign-out (the count is shown in the warning and the photos are lost whatever the person chooses), on remote wipe or emergency lock, and on self-expiry.
- The queue has its own budget `QUEUE_MAX_MB` (12), outside `CACHE_MAX_MB`. Cache eviction never touches queued items. If the browser evicts the queue anyway, the app says "N items were lost". The queue requests persistent storage.

Parameters and ownership:
- `QUEUE_MAX_AGE` is renamed `PAYOUT_PROVIDER_WAIT_MAX`. WAL owns it (24 h). SPD `payout_retry_window` references it. OPS-08 only cites it. The OPS-08 server-side "Queue" is money held as Pending, not the device queue.
- `PAYOUT_PENDING_ALERT_MINUTES` stays owned by OPS-13 (10 minutes proposed). XCT shows no number and the "30 minutes" text is removed.

Residual risk: non-XCT stories were checked from grep results and critic quotes, not read in full. Each epic owner must verify the exact wording in their stories when applying this decision.
